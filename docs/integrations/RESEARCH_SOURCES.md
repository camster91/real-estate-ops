- TRREB Strategic Plan 2026–2029: member technology list including REALM, Matrix, BrokerBay, SkySlope Forms/DigiSign, GeoWarehouse, MPAC propertyline and others.
- PropTx Online Data Agreements: IDX is available as a RESO API for participating/opted-in brokerage listing advertising under the agreement.
- REALTOR.ca DDF Web API: OAuth 2.0, server tokens, RESO Web API/OData, feed permissions.
- SkySlope Partnership API: OAuth 2.0, files, contacts, envelopes, signed documents, webhooks and related resources.
- RECO annual financial filing guidance: submissions began Oct 1, 2026; inaugural filing due Oct 30, 2026; monthly trust-reconciliation reporting planned for 2027.
- RECO brokerage administration resources and June 2026 inspection guidance: trade record sheets and monthly trust reconciliation are core brokerage controls.
- FINTRAC real-estate guidance: identity verification and recordkeeping, including applicable five-year retention requirements.
- OPC AI/privacy guidance: privacy-by-design, limit personal/sensitive information shared with generative AI.
- CRTC CASL guidance: consent, identification and unsubscribe requirements for commercial electronic messages.
