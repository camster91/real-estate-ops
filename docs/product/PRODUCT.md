# Product requirements

## Users
Owner/executive, broker of record, manager, agent, transaction coordinator, administrator, property manager, accounting/finance, employee, external partner, client, system agent.

## Primary jobs
- Receive and qualify leads.
- Maintain a complete relationship history.
- Manage properties and listings independently of transactions.
- Run repeatable buyer, seller, lease, commercial, referral, property-management and other service workflows.
- Track tasks, deadlines, approvals and dependencies.
- Track required documents, signatures and expiry.
- Maintain Ontario brokerage compliance evidence.
- Coordinate email/calendar activity without replacing email/calendar.
- Track commission and operational financial records without autonomously moving money.
- Surface risks and exceptions in one Operations Inbox.

## V1 success condition
A user can receive a lead, create/associate contacts and a property, open a transaction or service engagement, assign a team, execute a workflow, track documents/compliance/communications, reach closing/completion, calculate commission, and archive the operational record without maintaining a parallel spreadsheet.

## Non-goals for V1
- Replacing the brokerage's accounting ledger.
- Autonomous regulatory filing.
- Autonomous money movement.
- Replacing MLS systems.
- General-purpose unrestricted browser automation.
- AI making legal/compliance determinations.
